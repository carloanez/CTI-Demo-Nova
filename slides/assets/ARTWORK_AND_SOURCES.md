## Final cover prompt

+Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original keynote cover illustration for a cybersecurity conference presentation, widescreen 16:9.
Primary request: create a polished futuristic cyber investigator illustration inspired by an editorial conference aesthetic with warm ivory, matte black, and burnt orange. This is original art, not a copy of a conference logo or existing character.
Scene/backdrop: clean warm ivory (#F7F4ED) studio background with very subtle concentric technical orbit lines behind the subject.
Subject: a single anonymous futuristic cyber investigator in matte black sculpted protective armor and an opaque dark visor, restrained burnt-orange illuminated seams. The figure examines a small floating cluster of abstract evidence nodes and signal traces held near one hand, evoking threat intelligence becoming a detection.
Style/medium: premium 3D conceptual editorial render, refined surfaces, realistic light, confident and minimal, warm neutral studio lighting.
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 composition. All character and evidence detail should occupy only the RIGHTMOST 42% of the image. Show waist-up figure with head fully in frame, right of center. Keep the LEFT 58% completely empty uninterrupted ivory for large editable slide typography. Subtle grounding shadow. Upper left must stay blank.
Color palette: ivory background, charcoal black armor, restrained burnt orange (#CC752F) accents. No blue, neon green, or purple.
Constraints: no words, letters, numbers, logos, UI panels, watermarks, weapons, or additional characters. Do not embed any slide title. Ensure strong subject silhouette and useful empty left space.
